## Rules
<!-- Drafted from observed copy — TODO(user): confirm each. -->
- Lead with the failure story, then the fix. Problems are told in the customer's words ("Our website is down." You check. The domain expired.).
- Specifics over adjectives: "alerts 90, 60, 30, 14, 7, 3, and 1 day(s) before expiration", not "timely alerts".
- Say "you"; write like one founder talking to one owner ("I built DomainPilot…" in outreach).
- Low-pressure asks with an explicit out: "If it's not useful, just say so." / "One reply and I'm gone." <!-- marketing/outreach-emails-2026-07-07.md -->
- Insurance/safety-net metaphor is the house metaphor; use it, don't invent new ones.
- Short declarative sentences for emphasis ("It was perfect for about two weeks.").
